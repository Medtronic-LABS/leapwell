# LeapWell

LeapWell is the Medtronic Labs programme that gives community health workers an AI-assisted, offline-first
app (Apon Sushashthya) backed by a Frappe server, AI services and a micro-coaching platform.

This Wiki describes the project and its features. How we work is in the repository, not here.

## Start here

- [Project overview](Project-Overview): what LeapWell is, who uses it, how the pieces fit
- [Repos and areas](Repos-and-Areas): the seven code repos and the product areas they serve
- [Glossary](Glossary): programme and clinical terms used across the project

## Features

- [AI Scribe](Feature-AI-Scribe): voice to structured clinical fields
- [AI visit briefing](Feature-Visit-Briefing): pre-visit cards for the health worker
- [Micro-coaching](Feature-Micro-Coaching): coaching content and analytics for health workers
- [Visit flow and CDSS](Feature-Visit-Flow-and-CDSS): triage, assessment, risk bands and recommendations
- [Teleconsult (Shukhee)](Feature-Teleconsult-Shukhee): integration with the Shukhee calling system
- [Household and enrolment](Feature-Household-and-Enrolment): households, members and enrolment
- [Sync and offline](Feature-Sync-and-Offline): offline-first data and sync
